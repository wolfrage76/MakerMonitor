MakerMonitor Flasher

Install the MakerMonitor firmware on a color panel from your browser. Nothing to install.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. The page has to be opened over https (or from localhost) to use USB.
  1. Plug the panel into this computer with a USB-C data cable (some cables only charge).
  2. Pick your panel above, press Connect and install and choose the serial port (USB Serial or CH340 on a CrowPanel, USB JTAG/serial debug unit on the Waveshare boards and the Guition).
  3. On a first install, leave Erase device ticked. It takes a minute or two; keep the cable in until it says done.
  4. The panel restarts and asks for your Wi-Fi on screen. After that, updates install over Wi-Fi from the panel's Settings.

No port listed? Try another cable or USB port. If the panel is stuck, hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT, then connect again.
