# Surface: poller web dashboard

Scope: the LAN dashboard at the poller's `/` (Overview, Models, Activity, Export, Health, Settings). Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.
Audience and job: one MakerWorld creator checking, several times a day on desktop and phone, what changed since the last look and what to act on.
Constraints: single self-contained page, no external assets, light and dark from the OS, 12-hour times, every existing route, id, endpoint and the Kiosk link kept.

Grounded list (ranked): 1 filament spool labels and swatch cards; 2 parts-drawer cabinet labels; 3 a baseball line score; 4 an engineering drawing title block; 5 craft-fair price tags and table signs; 6 a print-shop receipt tally; 7 a workshop pegboard shadow board.
Challenger verdicts: wayfinding yellow, declined (kept: one colour reserved for one job); dense Japanese web, competitive on clarity (kept: hairline modules with a header strip instead of floating cards); orienteering map, declined (kept: fixed metric colours as terrain, one reserved colour for "now" and the active choice); catalog sleeve, declined (kept: restraint, one plot owns its panel); event catalog, declined; datamatics, declined (kept: one face for every number).
Unattended run: the user was away, so the assigned direction was built without a decision round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Today is a shadow board. Every metric keeps a fixed slot and every hour is a peg hole; yesterday's hour is the painted outline and today's peg fills it, so gaps read at a glance. Refuses the row of identical white stat tiles with sparklines.
OWN-WORLD: hardboard-brown shell band with folder tabs cut into a cool galvanized-steel ground; flat white panels on hairlines with a header strip, no drop shadows; one system sans with tabular figures for every number; shadow-paint orange reserved for what needs the creator and the current hour; metric colours unchanged from the panels.
STORY: the creator sees today against yesterday-by-now, then the short list of what needs them beside it, then models and trends below.
FIRST VIEWPORT: band with brand, account, status and tabs; left two thirds the Today board (one row per metric: count, change against yesterday by now, 24-peg day strip); right third "Needs you" with the action items. On phone the tabs become a bottom bar.
